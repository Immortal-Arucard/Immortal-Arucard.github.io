Downloadable Free Apps

These are the installable web apps published so far. Each one is free.

  • The World Government, Inc.

    FREE

    The World Government, Inc. publishes this website. Its work is arranged in divisions. Philosophy is the first and primary division.

    • Philosophy, Religion, Science, Recreation, Building, and Trading.
    • Installs as its own app from this site.
    Open / Install

    Chrome menu > Add to Home screen / Install app

  • DRP Compass

    FREE

    The Democratic-Republican Party Platform Test. Thirty proposals from the platform. For each one, choose whether you more agree or more disagree. Every liberal answer moves you one point right. Every conservative answer moves you one point left. Your result lands somewhere on a line from −30 to +30.

    • Standard: for each proposition, select whether you more agree or more disagree.
    • Deluxe: the same as Standard, plus a required sentiment strength score for each proposition, out of 100.
    • Sign in, My history, and Stats are on the app.
    Open / Install

    Chrome menu > Add to Home screen / Install app

  • DRP Platform

    FREE

    Thirty proposals, as the DRP Compass states them. Each section quotes the proposal, then gives the explanation he has stated for it, and the objections he has answered. Nothing here is taken from questions he left open. The liberal or conservative mark is the side the Compass gives to more agreement.

    • The thirty proposals, in the Compass’s own words.
    • A contents list for moving through those proposals.
    Open / Install

    Chrome menu > Add to Home screen / Install app

  • Chess

    FREE

    Chess is a division of Recreation. Bot chess is playable. Community is where you sign in and click Join Chess. The other sections are planned and are not operational.

    • Play a standard game against a bot on this site.
    • Community: sign in, then click Join Chess. Opening the page does not join Chess.
    • The other sections on the page are planned and are not operational.
    Open / Install

    Chrome menu > Add to Home screen / Install app

  • Taskmaster

    FREE

    Your personal task list. Plan a start, due time and duration, track the time you spend, and get reminders. Tasks are saved on this device and work offline. Signed in, they sync to your TWGI master account.

    • Not signed in, the list stays on this device.
    • Signed in, the same list, order, names, times, and tracking show on every device where you sign in. Offline changes sync when you are back online.
    • Reminders send only each task’s name and times. Sign in to let other TWGI accounts comment.
    Open / Install

    Chrome menu > Add to Home screen / Install app

  • Journal

    FREE

    Your private journal. Only you can see it, and it never appears on your public profile. Each TWGI master account keeps its own journal.

    • An entry has a date and time, an optional title, and the text. You can search your entries.
    • The Pensieve: tap Start, talk, then tap Stop, and fix the words before saving. The microphone is used only after you tap Start, for live text or for audio, one at a time.
    • Original dictation copies are ask, always, or never. The audio and unedited transcript stay on this device.
    Open / Install
