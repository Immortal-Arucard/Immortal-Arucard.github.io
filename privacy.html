Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

DRP Compass is operated by The World Government, Inc. You can take the test without an account. This page says what is stored when you do.

Your account

If you create an account, we store your email address and your name. Email sign-in uses a link we send you. Continue with Google is optional. If you use it, Google shares the email and name on that Google account, and we store those the same way.

Supabase stores the account and runs sign-in. Supabase is the processor. We do not sell personal data, and the site has no ads.

Test results

If you are signed in, your saved results are linked to your account. Each one stores the time, the score, the answers, which version you took (Standard or Deluxe), and any commentary you write. A Deluxe result also stores a strength from 1 to 100 for each answer.

If you are not signed in, finishing the test still stores an anonymous record: the time, the score, the answers, and whether it was Standard or Deluxe. A Deluxe record also stores the strengths. That record has no name, no email address, and no IP address.

A link you share encodes only the answers. It does not include your name or email.

Site visits

We count visits. Each visit stores the time, the page path, and a salted hash of the IP address. The raw IP address is not stored. The salt stays in the database so the hash is not reversible from the stored value alone.

Who can see it

Other people using the site cannot read your name, email, results, or commentary. The operator can access what is stored, including to delete an account when you ask. A private page shows the operator aggregate counts and scores. It does not publish a list of names or emails.

Deletion

Delete account removes your saved results and profile from the database. That button cannot delete the sign-in record itself. Email cubzub@gmail.com and ask us to delete your account. We will delete the account and the data linked to it.
