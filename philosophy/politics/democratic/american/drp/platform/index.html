Democratic-Republican Party

DRP Platform

Thirty proposals, as the DRP Compass states them. Each section quotes the proposal and restates only what that sentence says. The liberal or conservative mark is the side the Compass gives to more agreement.

1. Taxes

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Taxes are evil and unnecessary.

The proposal calls taxes evil. It also calls them unnecessary. Both judgments belong to this one stance.

2. Inflation/deflation

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Inflation is bad, and abundance- driven deflation is good.

The proposal calls inflation bad. It calls deflation good when that deflation is driven by abundance.

3. Money not hegemonic value

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Money should not be the hegemonic unit of value in society.

The proposal says money should not be the hegemonic unit of value in society. It rejects money as the ruling measure of value.

4. Free speech

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Free speech must be absolute, short of explicitly wishing for or celebrating death or bodily harm upon a person or a people.

The proposal says free speech must be absolute. The only limit it names is explicitly wishing for, or celebrating, death or bodily harm upon a person or a people.

5. Abortion & contraception

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Abortion, including contraceptives and all forms of pregnancy prevention or negation, should be prohibited, except to prevent the death of the mother.

The proposal prohibits abortion. It includes contraceptives in that prohibition, and all forms of pregnancy prevention or negation. The exception it names is preventing the death of the mother.

6. Immigration

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

We should enable unlimited legal immigration by controlling immigrants in a strong, authoritarian way.

The proposal calls for unlimited legal immigration. It also calls for immigrants to be controlled in a strong, authoritarian way.

7. American Monarchy & voting

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

We should adopt an American Monarchy, and greatly restrict the right to vote and the democratic mechanisms of our society.

The proposal calls for an American Monarchy. It calls for the right to vote to be greatly restricted, and for the democratic mechanisms of society to be greatly restricted.

8. Climate & environment

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

The climate and the environment should be protected using a reasonable level of coercion.

The proposal says the climate and the environment should be protected. The means it names is a reasonable level of coercion.

9. Deistic God

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

We believe that science and logic have proven the existence of a deistic God.

The proposal states a belief: that science and logic have proven the existence of a deistic God.

10. Nonlethal interventionism

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

In foreign policy, we should adopt a stance of hyper-agressive, nonlethal interventionism.

The proposal sets the foreign-policy stance as hyper-agressive, nonlethal interventionism. That is the spelling and the pairing given in the text.

11. Human inequality

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

We should challenge and debunk the anti-scientific lie that people are equal to one another.

The proposal calls the claim that people are equal to one another an anti-scientific lie. It says that lie should be challenged and debunked.

12. Tariffs

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Tariffs for the purpose of stimulating an abundance of production are a moral good.

The proposal calls tariffs a moral good when their purpose is stimulating an abundance of production. The judgment is tied to that purpose.

13. Prisoner freedoms

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Prisoners should be free to work a normal job for a normal competitive wage, purchase and use land and property normally, and spend unlimited amounts of time with any friends, family, or others, who wish to spend that time with the prisoner.

The proposal says prisoners should be free to work a normal job for a normal competitive wage. They should be free to purchase and use land and property normally. They should be free to spend unlimited time with any friends, family, or others who wish to spend that time with the prisoner.

14. Evidence standards & jury trials

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Judicial standards of evidence lower than beyond a reasonable doubt, and non-jury trials, should be abolished, both criminally and civilly.

The proposal would abolish judicial standards of evidence lower than beyond a reasonable doubt. It would abolish non-jury trials. It applies both abolitions criminally and civilly.

15. Police & no lethal force

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

We must hire many more police, pay them much better, and abolish the use of lethal force in law enforcement.

The proposal says many more police must be hired, and that they must be paid much better. It says the use of lethal force in law enforcement must be abolished.

16. Humans naturally wicked

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Resolved: humans are naturally wicked.

The proposal is a resolution: humans are naturally wicked.

17. Globalism vs nationalism

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Globalism is superior to nationalism.

The proposal says globalism is superior to nationalism.

18. Property rights

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Except for slavery, the right to own and control physical property shall not be infringed.

The proposal says the right to own and control physical property shall not be infringed. The exception it names is slavery.

19. Slavery condemned

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Slavery shall forever be illegal and condemned.

The proposal says slavery shall forever be illegal, and that it shall forever be condemned.

20. Socialism & Communism

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Socialism and Communism are evil to the extent that they are funded coersively or usurp the authority of the state.

The proposal says Socialism and Communism are evil to the extent that they are funded coersively, or to the extent that they usurp the authority of the state. The text limits the judgment to that extent, and it spells the word coersively.

21. Drug legalization

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

All drugs should be legalized and regulated.

The proposal says all drugs should be legalized, and that they should be regulated.

22. Suicide illegal

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Suicide should be illegal.

The proposal says suicide should be illegal.

23. Immortality & resurrection

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Immortality and resurrection are the highest, most noble aspirations of humanity.

The proposal names immortality and resurrection as the highest, most noble aspirations of humanity.

24. Weapons legal

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

All weapons should be legal and reasonably regulated.

The proposal says all weapons should be legal, and that they should be reasonably regulated.

25. Polygyny & birthrates

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Society should embrace polyginy and all other means of maximizing birthrates.

The proposal says society should embrace polyginy, and all other means of maximizing birthrates. Polyginy is the spelling in the text.

26. Gay

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

It is wrong to be gay.

The proposal says it is wrong to be gay.

27. Trans

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

With the current technology, it is wrong to be trans.

The proposal says that, with the current technology, it is wrong to be trans. The judgment is stated together with that condition.

28. Prostitution

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as liberal.

Prostitution should be legal and regulated.

The proposal says prostitution should be legal, and that it should be regulated.

29. Nonlethal military

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

Our military should exclusively use nonlethal force.

The proposal says the military should exclusively use nonlethal force.

30. Death penalty abolition

On the DRP Compass, more agreement counts as conservative.

The death penalty shall be prohibited in all circumstances.

The proposal says the death penalty shall be prohibited in all circumstances. It states no exception.
